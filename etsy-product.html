Questions before you start?

What exactly do I receive after purchasing?

You receive the Etsy Mission Planner HTML file plus a Quick Start PDF explaining how to open, use, and save your planner data.

Download → Click the HTML file → Start using your planner.

Do I need to install any software?

No. The planner is an HTML file that opens directly in your web browser. No special software or complicated installation is required.

How does the Etsy Mission Planner work?

You first provide information about your goals, experience, starting point, available time, and desired timeline. The planner then structures your Etsy journey into a day-by-day roadmap with specific missions and actions.

What is included in each daily mission?

Each mission can include a clear objective, action tasks, a checklist, instructions, common mistakes to avoid, an expected outcome, the next step, and supporting guidance where available.

So you do not just see what to do; you understand how to approach the task and what comes next.

Is this another Etsy course?

No. The focus is execution rather than hours of passive learning. Instead of constantly watching another tutorial and wondering what to do next, you use the planner to follow your daily mission and take action.

Where is my planner data stored?

Your planner is designed to work locally in your browser. The included Quick Start guide explains how to save and back up your data so you do not accidentally lose your progress.

Does it guarantee Etsy sales?

No. The planner does not guarantee sales, revenue, or profit. It gives you a structured system for planning, executing, tracking, and improving your Etsy work. Your results depend on your actions, products, market, and other factors.
